Privacy

This page is for members of the Discord server that uses this verification service. The service does handle personal data. This page says what it handles, who else is involved and how long the data stays.

What is processed

  • Your Discord user ID.
  • Your account creation time, which is derived from that ID.
  • The time you joined the server.
  • Your role and restriction state in the server.
  • The outcome of your verification.

How long it is kept

Session data is short-lived and lasts about 15 minutes. Audit records and private log messages have a 30-day retention target. Removal can be delayed if cleanup fails, and cleanup is retried.

Discord sign-in

Discord sign-in requests only the identify scope, which is used to confirm that you are the member who asked to verify. Tokens are used in memory and are not stored. Revoking them is attempted after the identity check.

Cloudflare Turnstile

Cloudflare Turnstile runs a challenge in your browser. To validate the result, your IP address may be sent to Cloudflare Siteverify.

Cloudflare and Discord

Cloudflare and Discord process request, browser and account information under their own policies. Their backups and logs have their own retention, which this service does not control.

What this service does not collect

  • It does not collect your birth date or any identity document.
  • It does not fingerprint your browser.
  • It does not keep a database of IP history.
  • It does not keep a database of alternate accounts.

What verification means

Passing verification grants the ordinary member role. It is not a guarantee about a person. Account age means the time elapsed since your Discord account was created. It is not your age.

Deleting your data

To ask for deletion, contact server staff through Help. Staff cancel any pending admission and erase the matching app data and log copies. Deletion does not by itself remove your access to the server, and it does not affect data held by Discord or Cloudflare. If you are a staff member, records of actions you took on other members stay until their normal 30-day deletion, so those actions remain accountable.
