Privacy

What the verification service of this Discord server handles, who else is involved and how long the data stays.

What is processed

  • Your Discord user ID, and your account creation time, which comes from that ID
  • When you joined the server, and your roles and restrictions there
  • The outcome of your verification
  • Your username & avatar, shown in the private log but not stored here

How long it is kept

Session data lasts about 15 minutes. Audit records and private log messages are kept for 30 days, a failed cleanup is retried, which can delay removal.

Discord sign-in

Sign-in only asks for the identify scope, to confirm you are the member who started verification. Tokens are only used in memory, never stored, and the service tries to revoke them after the check.

Cloudflare & Discord

Cloudflare Turnstile runs the browser check, and your IP address may be sent to Cloudflare to validate it. Cloudflare and Discord process request, browser and account data under their own policies, including their own backups and logs, which this service doesn't control.

Deleting your data

Ask staff through Help, they cancel any pending verification and erase your app data and log copies. Your server access stays as it is, and data held by Discord or Cloudflare isn't affected. If you are staff, records of actions you took on other members stay until their normal 30 day deletion, so those actions stay accountable.
